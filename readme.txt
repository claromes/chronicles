Explore the Dragonscape Chronicles: text-based adventure game. Written to learn C++.

"... stands still and ponders."

$ gcc -o chronicles chronicles.cpp -lstdc++

HISTORY:

The player, who is exploring new places, finds a scroll and discovers the map of three locations. Each location has a dragon that imprisons harvesters, who work to gather the food that feeds each dragon. The player needs to help the harvesters finish their work so that the dragons can feed on the entire harvest, sleep, and the harvesters can escape.

Since the harvesters can't gather everything in a single period, the dragons never fall asleep.

There are two options to win the game: assist in all harvests in the same period or complete the inventory.

CHARACTERS:

- Main player with their scroll
- Three Dragons: Greenish, Bluish, and Yellowish
- Harvesters

ENVIRONMENTS:

- Enchanted Forest
- Crystal Caverns
- Mystic Peaks

CHALLENGES:

- Assist in the harvest of the three locations. It is necessary to complete all three harvests in a full period; otherwise, if a new period begins, a new harvest must be started. Each period lasts 10 minutes in real-time.

- Complete the inventory with the 10 items that can be found during the journey to exchange with the dragons in return for the freedom of the harvesters.

INVENTORY ITEMS:

- Magic Wood
- Enchanted Sickle
- Green Flower
- Blue Flower
- Yellow Flower
- Diamond
